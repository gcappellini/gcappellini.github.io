### Dimensione Brama

A collective combining theatre, music, and dance in innovative performances. I was session drummer for indie-pop singers and joined Ernesto e i Soci for the tour of their album "Un Giorno Sì Un Giorno No" (2018). We later released "Ingresso Riservato" (2023) under the band name Soloperisoci.
